## Field-Adjusted Source Quality

The clinical evidence hierarchy in the deep-research protocol does not map
cleanly onto empirical AI systems. The following field adjustment is used:

- Level III: controlled benchmark or comparative systems study.
- Level VI: descriptive benchmark/database paper.
- Grade B: peer-reviewed or formally accepted with an inspectable method.
- Grade C: relevant preprint without verified peer review.

| Source group | Evidence level | Grade | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reflexion, ExpeL, AutoGuide, AutoManual, Agent-Pro, Voyager, LearnAct, Agent Workflow Memory, Memp, ReasoningBank, Skill-Pro, LEGOMem, SWE-bench, SWE-agent | III | B | Include as primary related work |
| ICE, SkillWeaver | III | C | Include with explicit preprint caveat |
| PyBugHive, BugsInPy | VI | B | Include as benchmark provenance |

No predatory-venue indicators were found. Method authors evaluate systems they
created, so an ordinary intellectual conflict is present across most method
papers; this is handled by treating reported gains as paper-specific evidence
rather than independently replicated facts.
